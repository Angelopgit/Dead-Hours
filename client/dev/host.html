Night clock on this device

This page runs a real night for four survivors in a worker, the way a player host's browser does. Leave it running, switch to another tab or minimise the window for a minute, then come back and read the rows marked hidden. 60 steps a second with a wait near 17 ms means the night keeps going; about 1 a second means this browser holds a hidden tab's worker back.
